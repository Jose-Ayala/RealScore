How To Use RealScore

Use this guide to evaluate a market, tune assumptions, and interpret the decision outputs quickly.

1) Quick Start Workflow

  1. Choose a metro in the Deal Inputs panel.
  2. Enter the target listing price.
  3. Adjust expense ratio, down payment, and interest rate to match your financing assumptions.
  4. Optionally set Vacancy Shock and Rent Drawdown, then turn on Apply stress scenario.
  5. Review Deal Score, Cap Rate, Cash-on-Cash, IRR range, and score breakdown bars.
  6. Use the Scenario Snapshot chart to compare base versus stress outcomes.

2) Deal Inputs Explained

Search Metro + Metro Market

Type part of a city or state in Search Metro to filter the dropdown, then select the best matching metro market.

Undervalued only

Shows only markets where ZHVI is at or above your listing price so you can focus on potentially discounted opportunities.

Listing Price

The purchase price used for valuation and return calculations. This directly affects discount/premium versus ZHVI and cap rate.

Expense Ratio

Estimated operating expenses as a percent of rent (maintenance, insurance, property taxes, etc.). Higher values lower NOI and returns.

Down Payment + Interest Rate

Used to compute annual debt service and cash-on-cash return. More down payment usually improves cash-on-cash stability.

Vacancy Shock + Rent Drawdown

Stress inputs only applied when Apply stress scenario is on. They model downside income conditions.

3) Understanding Outputs

Deal Score (0-100)

Composite score based on Valuation (40%), Income (35%), and Risk (25%).

  • 80 to 100: Strong opportunity
  • 60 to 79: Viable with caution
  • 0 to 59: Higher-risk profile

Cap Rate

NOI divided by listing price. This is your unlevered income yield.

Cash-on-Cash

Annual cash flow after debt service divided by down payment amount.

IRR Estimate Range

A modeled low-to-high return range using income plus forecast appreciation (ZHVF).

Projected Monthly Rent

Based on metro ZORI, adjusted down when stress is active.

4) Baseline Cards And Tooltips

The four baseline cards show the source signals used in scoring:

  • ZHVI Baseline: metro home value benchmark
  • ZORI Baseline: metro rent benchmark
  • ZHVF Growth: short-term forecast appreciation
  • Market Risk Inputs: inventory and days-to-pending

Click the small i icon on each card for a plain-language explanation.

5) Scenario Snapshot Chart

The chart compares Base and Stress values side-by-side for Cap Rate, Cash-on-Cash, and IRR midpoint.

Use it to quickly judge downside sensitivity before committing to a market or price.

6) Recommended User Flow

  1. Set listing price first.
  2. Turn on Undervalued only and shortlist 3-5 metros.
  3. Calibrate financing inputs to your likely loan terms.
  4. Check base-case metrics and keep only opportunities with acceptable cap rate and cash-on-cash.
  5. Enable stress scenario and reject deals that collapse under modest shocks.
  6. Use score breakdown bars to identify whether weakness is valuation, income, or risk-driven.

Important Note

RealScore is a decision-support tool and not financial advice. Validate assumptions, run due diligence, and consult professionals before investing.
